## Known limits (documented in-app and in `docs/security/security-model.md`)
- Append-only protection on `notes` and `audit_events` is an application-level integrity control
  enforced via repository semantics and SQLite triggers; it is not tamper-proof against direct
  edits to the SQLite file unless database files and backups are externally secured and validated.
